## Terms

A nested page. Its path must stay nested all the way through — `legal/terms.md` in the IR becomes
the page path `legal/terms`, the file `content/legal/terms.md`, and the route `/legal/terms/`. A
collector that flattened it would put a stray `/terms` at the top of the site instead.
